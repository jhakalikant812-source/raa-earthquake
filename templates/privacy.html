Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2026

Welcome to the RAA Earthquake Monitoring App. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Email address during account signup.
  • Location data (latitude and longitude) with your permission.
  • Basic usage data to improve the app.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Detect earthquakes near your location.
  • Send email alerts if an earthquake occurs within 100 km.
  • Improve the earthquake monitoring experience.

3. Location Permission

Our app may request location permission. This location data is used only to detect earthquakes near you and send alerts. Your location is never publicly shared.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. Your personal data is stored securely and is never sold or shared with third parties.

5. Earthquake Data Source

Earthquake data displayed in this application comes from publicly available sources such as the USGS (United States Geological Survey).

6. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated occasionally. Any updates will be posted on this page.
