    Earthquake Education

    Earthquakes occur when energy stored within the Earth's crust is suddenly released. This usually happens due to movement of tectonic plates along fault lines.

    How Earthquakes Are Measured

    Scientists measure earthquakes using instruments called seismographs. The magnitude scale indicates the strength of an earthquake.

    Earthquake Safety Tips

    • Stay calm during shaking
    • Drop, cover, and hold on
    • Stay away from windows
    • If outdoors, move to an open area
