Earthquake Safety Tips: What to Do Before, During & After

Earthquakes can happen suddenly and without warning, making it important for everyone to know how to respond. Proper preparation and quick action can save lives and reduce injuries during an earthquake.

Before an Earthquake (Preparation)

Preparation is the most important step in earthquake safety. Being ready in advance can significantly reduce risks.

  • Prepare an emergency kit with water, food, torch, and first aid
  • Secure heavy furniture like cupboards and shelves
  • Identify safe spots in your home (under strong tables)
  • Keep emergency contact numbers ready

During an Earthquake (Immediate Action)

When an earthquake starts, stay calm and act quickly.

  • Drop, Cover, and Hold On
  • Stay away from windows and glass
  • Do not use elevators
  • If outdoors, move to an open area away from buildings

After an Earthquake

Even after the shaking stops, danger may still exist.

  • Check yourself and others for injuries
  • Stay away from damaged buildings
  • Be prepared for aftershocks
  • Follow official instructions from authorities

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Running outside during shaking
  • Standing near windows or balconies
  • Ignoring safety instructions

Why Earthquake Awareness Matters

Many injuries during earthquakes happen due to panic and lack of awareness. Education and preparedness can greatly reduce damage and save lives.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What should I do first during an earthquake?
Drop, cover, and hold on to protect yourself.

Is it safe to run outside?
No, it can be dangerous due to falling objects.

What are aftershocks?
Smaller earthquakes that occur after the main shock.
